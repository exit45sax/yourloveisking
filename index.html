The Show

Faithful to the records.
Alive in the room.

Your Love is King - a Tribute to Sade recreates Sade’s sound the way the records were made: restrained, warm and exact. Silky bass, live tenor saxophone, and a rich, smoky voice made for these songs.

From Your Love Is King and The Sweetest Taboo to No Ordinary Love, Smooth Operator and Soldier of Love, it’s a night of the songs audiences came to hear.

The Band

Five players. One sound.

Tap a photo to read each bio.

Justine Michele is a Philadelphia-based vocalist known for her sultry tone, captivating stage presence, and exceptional versatility. She has built a strong reputation throughout the Philadelphia and tri-state entertainment markets, bringing a distinctive sound and magnetic energy to every stage.

She is the lead vocalist of Your Love is King - a Tribute to Sade, and Milan 77, an EBE entertainment band. With a natural vocal likeness to Sade and influences ranging from Donna Summer to Chappell Roan, Justine’s artistry spans soulful R&B, timeless classics, contemporary pop and beyond.

Her career highlights include opening for Monica and Keith Sweat, sharing the stage with Sister Sledge, and performing at exclusive private events, including a ceremony honoring Bill Gates hosted by the Horatio Alger Association. With a signature sound and genuine connection to her audience, Justine delivers a memorable musical experience with every performance.

Ron Heller is the founder and bandleader of Your Love is King - a Tribute to Sade. Born and raised in the Philly area, Ron grew up listening to all the great music in the region and was always captivated by the sound of Sade. After working with Justine in another band, Ron knew that one day Justine would be the perfect lead in a Sade tribute band. Fast forward several years, when desire met opportunity, and Your Love is King - a Tribute to Sade was born! Ron brings his unique talents as a multi-instrumentalist to the project, performing on bass, saxophone and vocals.

Ron has performed with artists such as the late Grover Washington, Jr. and trumpeter Arturo Sandoval, and has toured with the world-famous Glenn Miller Orchestra as a sax and woodwind player and a cappella vocalist. He is also the bandleader of the duo LakeHouse, performing over 65 shows a year.

Roosevelt Walker is a staple in the Philadelphia scene, currently performing in a variety of tribute bands, from Santana to Chicago, as well as his original smooth jazz project, Exit45. Roosevelt brings the perfect flavor to the project!

He is a member of several active Philadelphia tribute bands, including the Inca Band - a Tribute to Santana and Chicago 9 - the Music of Chicago. He is also an experienced music engineer and producer, having recorded and mixed many albums for other artists.

Anil brings his years of experience as a keyboardist, bandleader, and entrepreneur to Your Love is King - a Tribute to Sade. He is the bandleader of The Yacht Kingz, a nine-piece yacht rock tribute band performing 1970s and 1980s soft rock classics, and a highly in-demand keyboardist in the Philadelphia region.

Ben Cuyler is a professional live drummer and bandleader active in the Mid-Atlantic R&B, soul, and cover music scenes, spanning Philadelphia, New Jersey, Delaware, New York City, and Washington, D.C.

He has performed live on drums for R&B singer Keke Wyatt, including shows at City Winery in Philadelphia, and is a frequent performer with regional groups and vocalists such as Legacy, Showtyme, Roy Richardson, and Grace Little.
