The Show

Not an impression.
A reverence.

Your Love is King - a Tribute to Sade recreates the sound of Sade the way the records were made — restrained, warm and exact. Direct-to-board bass, tenor saxophone, and a contralto voice built for this catalogue.

From Your Love Is King and The Sweetest Taboo to No Ordinary Love, Smooth Operator and Soldier of Love — two sets of the songs audiences came to hear.
